## 1. Scope and Compliance

This Privacy Policy describes how AiMediaTank ("the Platform," "Service," "we," "us," or "our"), located at https://www.aimediatank.com, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Platform.

This policy applies to all users worldwide and is designed to comply with applicable data protection regulations, including but not limited to:

- **GDPR** — EU General Data Protection Regulation
- **UK DPA 2018** — UK Data Protection Act 2018
- **CCPA / CPRA** — California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act
- **COPPA** — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (U.S.)
- **LGPD** — Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados
- **PIPEDA** — Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act
- Other applicable national, state, and local privacy laws

This Privacy Policy should be read alongside our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md), which governs your use of the Platform.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Full name, legal name, chosen username, email address, phone number, and date of birth (used for age verification)
- **Profile Information:** Avatar/profile picture, biography, and location
- **Payment and Billing:** Payment method details, billing address, and transaction history (processed securely through Stripe; card details are never stored on our servers)
- **Content:** Media you upload, including metadata, descriptions, tags, AI tool disclosures, and associated prompts
- **Communications:** Messages to other users, comments, ratings, reviews, support requests, and correspondence with our team
- **Parental Consent Records:** For users aged 13–17, records of parental or guardian consent

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use the Platform, we automatically collect:

- **Device and Browser Information:** Device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution (via user-agent parsing)
- **Network Information:** IP address and approximate geolocation derived from your IP address
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, content viewed, features used, search queries, referring/exit URLs, and timestamps
- **Transaction Data:** Upload, purchase, download, and subscription activity
- **Session Data:** Session duration, interaction patterns, clicks, and scrolling behavior
- **Cookies and Tracking Technologies:** See Section 7 for details

### 3.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Social Login Providers:** When you sign in via Google, Microsoft, Facebook, or Apple, we may receive your name, email address, and profile picture as authorized by you during the login flow
- **Payment Processors:** Payment status, transaction confirmations, and fraud signals from Stripe
- **Fraud Prevention Services:** Identity verification and fraud detection data from our service providers

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Encryption at Rest:** Data stored in Azure services is encrypted at rest
- **Password Security:** Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with appropriate salt rounds
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access controls and the principle of least privilege
- **Security Assessments:** Regular vulnerability testing and security reviews
- **Threat Monitoring:** Automated threat detection and monitoring systems
- **Incident Response:** Documented incident response procedures and breach notification processes compliant with GDPR Article 33/34, CCPA, and other applicable regulations

**No system is 100% secure.** While we strive to protect your information using commercially reasonable measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials and notifying us immediately of any unauthorized access.

## 10. Children's Privacy

AiMediaTank is committed to protecting the privacy of minors.

### 10.1 Users Under 13

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
- The Platform is not intended for and may not be used by children under 13.
- If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@aimediatank.com. We will take steps to delete such information promptly.

### 10.2 Users Aged 13–17

- We collect personal information from users aged 13–17 **only with verifiable parental or guardian consent**.
- Parents and guardians have the right to:
  - Review the personal information we have collected from their child
  - Request deletion of their child's personal information
  - Refuse further collection or use of their child's information
  - Request restriction of their child's access to certain features
- To exercise any of these parental rights, contact support@aimediatank.com with the subject "Parental Request."

### 10.3 Protections for Minors

- We do **not** use personal information from users under 18 for targeted advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.
- Content filtering is automatically applied to users aged 13–17 and cannot be disabled by the minor.
- We do not share personal information of users under 18 with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 11. Data Retention

### 11.1 Content Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Uploaded content | Retained while your account is active |
| Purchased content | Remains available in your account while hosted on the Platform |
| Sales records | Retained as transaction records |

### 11.2 User Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account information (profile, settings) | Retained while account is active; deleted within 30 days of account termination |
| Transaction and financial records | **7 years** (legal and tax compliance requirements) |
| Usage and activity logs | **90 days** |
| Support communications | **2 years** |
| Parental consent records | Duration of account plus **3 years** |
| Security and fraud investigation logs | **2 years** |

### 11.3 Backup and Recovery

- Regular automated backups are performed for disaster recovery and business continuity.
- Backups are encrypted at rest and retained for **30 days**.
- Data deleted from production systems is purged from backups within **60 days**.

### 11.4 Right to Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 9). We will honor deletion requests within the timeframes required by applicable law, subject to our legal retention obligations (e.g., tax records, fraud prevention, ongoing disputes).

## 12. International Data Transfers

### 12.1 Global Service

AiMediaTank operates as a global service. Our primary infrastructure is hosted on **Microsoft Azure** with data centers in the United States and other regions. By using the Platform, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in the United States and other countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

### 12.2 Transfer Safeguards

When we transfer personal data across borders, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** EU-approved SCCs for transfers from the EU/EEA to countries without an adequacy decision
- **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):** In place with all third-party service providers who process personal data on our behalf
- **Azure Certifications:** Microsoft Azure maintains compliance with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3, and other international standards
- **Stripe Compliance:** Stripe maintains PCI DSS Level 1 certification and global data protection commitments
- **Encryption:** All cross-border data transfers are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest

### 12.3 Regional Details

**EU/EEA/UK Users:**
- Your data is processed in accordance with the GDPR and UK Data Protection Act 2018.
- International transfers are protected by SCCs and supplementary measures.
- Our processing activities are documented in a Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) as required by GDPR Article 30.
- You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

**California Residents:**
- Categories of personal information collected are described in Section 3.
- We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf.

**Brazil Residents:**
- Your data is processed in accordance with the LGPD.
- You have the right to petition the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

**Canada Residents:**
- Your data is processed in accordance with PIPEDA.
- You have the right to access, correct, and challenge the accuracy of your personal information.
- You may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## 13. Third-Party Services and Social Platforms

### 13.1 Social Login Providers

When you sign in via **Google**, **Microsoft**, **Facebook**, or **Apple**:
- We receive limited profile information (name, email, profile picture) as authorized by you.
- We do not gain access to your posts, friend lists, or private messages.
- You can revoke our access at any time through the provider's account settings.
- Each provider's own privacy policy governs how they process your data.

### 13.2 Social Sharing

When you share content to third-party platforms, those platforms collect data according to their own privacy policies. Our sharing features use:
- **YouTube:** Share links — governed by Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **TikTok:** Share links — governed by TikTok's [Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **X (formerly Twitter):** Intent-based URLs — governed by X's [Privacy Policy](https://x.com/en/privacy)

We do not receive data from these platforms when you share content to them. The share action redirects you to the third-party platform, which may collect data about you according to their own policies.

### 13.3 Payment Processor

**Stripe** processes all payments. When you make a transaction:
- Your payment details are sent directly to Stripe and are not stored on our servers.
- Stripe's [Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) governs how they handle your payment data.
- We receive only transaction confirmations, payment status, and fraud signals from Stripe.

### 13.4 Cloud Infrastructure

**Microsoft Azure** provides our cloud hosting, storage, and database services:
- All data is processed within Azure's infrastructure with enterprise-grade security.
- Azure's compliance certifications include ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3, HIPAA, and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
- Azure's [Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) provides additional details.
